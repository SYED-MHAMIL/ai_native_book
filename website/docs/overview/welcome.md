# Welcome to robot.ai

Welcome to robot.ai, a comprehensive learning platform for humanoid robotics education. This platform combines ROS 2, simulation, AI, and multimodal systems to provide you with a complete learning experience.

## Learning Journey

This guided learning experience will take you through four comprehensive modules:

1. **Module 1: The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Learn about middleware for robot control, including ROS 2 nodes, topics, services, and the rclpy Python bridge for humanoid robots.

2. **Module 2: The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)** - Explore physics simulation and environments, including gravity and collision simulation, high-fidelity Unity interaction, and sensor simulation.

3. **Module 3: The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)** - Discover perception and motion intelligence with Isaac Sim synthetic data, Isaac ROS accelerated VSLAM and navigation, and Nav2 for humanoid path planning.

4. **Module 4: Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Understand multimodal reasoning and behavior, and the role of VLA in humanoid interaction.

Each module is designed as a slide-style experience to guide you through the concepts in a structured way. Click on "Documentation" in the navigation bar to begin your learning journey!
