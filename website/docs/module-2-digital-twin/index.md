# The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)

Welcome to the second module of your robotics learning journey. In this module, you'll explore digital twins through physics simulation and environments using Gazebo and Unity.

## Overview

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical robot that allows for testing, simulation, and validation of robotic systems without the risks and costs associated with physical testing.

## What You'll Learn

- Physics and collision simulation in Gazebo
- High-fidelity Unity interaction
- Sensor simulation (LiDAR, Depth, IMU)
- Environment design and physics modeling
