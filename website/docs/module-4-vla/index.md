# Vision-Language-Action (VLA)

Welcome to the fourth and final module of your robotics learning journey. In this module, you'll explore Vision-Language-Action models, which represent the cutting edge of multimodal reasoning and behavior in robotics.

## Overview

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models represent a new paradigm in robotics where perception, language understanding, and action execution are integrated into unified models that can understand complex commands and execute them in real-world environments.

## What You'll Learn

- VLA concept overview and architecture
- The role of VLA in humanoid interaction
- Real-world applications and use cases
- Integration with perception and control systems
